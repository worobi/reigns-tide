Shine On Reign

Oh my beautiful firecracker Reign. Mommy loves you so much. And I miss you more and more everyday. I am so proud of you and blessed to call you my baby.

There is an emptiness inside my heart, which I'm scared will never be filled the way you filled it. But I know you are up there with our father GOD, and your grandma Mary, which gives me great comfort.

Reign if only you could see how many people you have been an inspiration to, it's incredible. You have so many people who love you, and who cover you in prayer.

Babygirl you are in fact Spider man! And not only did you inspire the world you saved the world baby!!!!
